# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place anyone can contribute to, regardless of
experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Critique the code, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and say why.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or publishing someone's
private information.

## Reporting

Open an issue, or email the maintainer if it is sensitive. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits and contributions that
breach this code, and may ban repeat offenders.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), v2.1.
